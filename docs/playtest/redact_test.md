# The redact-the-names test

For each line write who said it: **R** (Sir Rivet, the armour), **V** (Lady Varnish, the portrait) or **P** (Pendle, the pendulum). Names and rooms are blacked out. Don't look at the key until both rounds are done. About 10 minutes.
